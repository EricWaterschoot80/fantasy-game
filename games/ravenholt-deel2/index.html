Whispers of Ravenholt Part 2 — free 16-bit point-and-click castle adventure

Play Whispers of Ravenholt Part 2, the free sequel to Whispers of Ravenholt, in your browser with no download. Finn has slipped into the castle of Eldoria, where his father is held captive. Forge the fallen hero's sword at the smithy, lower a rope down the old well, win the princess's trust and find the secret gate beside the lion fountain.
