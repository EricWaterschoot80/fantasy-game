More about our free point-and-click adventure games

How to play

Every game is a point-and-click adventure, the kind of game people loved in the 80s and 90s: explore drawn scenes, pick up things, talk to characters and solve puzzles. No fast reflexes needed, just curiosity. Each adventure is made by hand by Eric Waterschoot.

Tips for parents: which game suits your child

  1. Pick a place

    Tap a sign on the map. Its hero walks there and tells you about the adventure.

  2. Press Play free

    The game opens straight away. On a phone, turn it sideways for the best view.

  3. Look, tap and solve

    Tap things to look at them, collect items in your bag and use them in the right spot.

Questions from parents and players

Are the games really free?

Yes. Every game on RetroAdventureWorld is completely free to play online in your browser, with no download and no sign-up.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. The games run in the browser on phones, tablets and desktops. On a phone they play best in landscape, and you can add them to your home screen as an app.

Which game should I start with?

Maanhoef (about 20 minutes) and The Amulet of Emberfall (about 15 minutes) are easy and suit players from about 7. Whispers of Ravenholt is a two-part mystery for ages 9 and up: play Part 1 first, then Part 2.

Are the games also for adults?

Yes. Children from about 7 can play them, and adults who grew up with classic point-and-click adventures will recognise the style. Younger children can play along with a parent who reads the text aloud.

What is a point-and-click adventure?

A classic style of game where you explore drawn scenes, collect and combine items, talk to characters and solve puzzles to move the story forward. No fast reflexes needed, just curiosity.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play every game without one. In Whispers of Ravenholt, an optional free account saves your progress in the cloud, so you can continue on another device.
